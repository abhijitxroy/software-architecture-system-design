

# Introduction

## What is UPI?

The **Unified Payments Interface (UPI)** is a real-time, interoperable payment system developed by the **National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI)**. It enables instant bank-to-bank money transfers using a mobile device, without requiring users to share bank account numbers or IFSC codes for every transaction.

UPI provides a common payment infrastructure that allows customers of participating banks to send and receive money 24×7 using a Virtual Payment Address (VPA), QR code, mobile number, or bank account details.

---

## Why was UPI introduced?

Before UPI, digital payments in India relied on systems such as NEFT, RTGS, IMPS, mobile wallets, debit cards, and credit cards. Although effective, these systems had several limitations:

- Bank account details had to be shared for every transfer.
- Different banks offered different user experiences.
- Merchant onboarding was relatively complex.
- Payment interoperability was limited.
- Some systems were not available around the clock or were designed for specific use cases.

UPI was introduced to provide a simple, secure, interoperable, and real-time payment platform for everyone.

---

## Key Objectives

- Instant bank-to-bank transfers.
- 24×7 availability.
- Interoperability across banks and payment apps.
- Simple user experience.
- Secure authentication using UPI PIN.
- Low-cost digital payment infrastructure.
- Support for individuals, merchants, and businesses.

---

## Key Characteristics

- Real-time payments.
- Mobile-first architecture.
- Open ecosystem for banks and approved payment service providers.
- Supports Person-to-Person (P2P) and Person-to-Merchant (P2M) transactions.
- Highly scalable national payment infrastructure.
